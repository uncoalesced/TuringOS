TuringOS · Design language 01 / 10
v1.0 · 26 Oct 2026 · built on the renewed palette

Black is the room.
Orange is the signal.

The visual language for TuringOS, the Debian-based OS where Claude agents work in sandboxes and nothing lands until you approve it. Six swatches and one gradient. Everything else is a tint of those.

01
Black is the room
Primary black holds every surface. Chrome stays grayscale so what you are looking at is the work.
02
Orange is the signal
One orange moment per view: the live thing. An agent working, the primary action, the focus. Never decoration.
03
Nothing lands unreviewed
States follow the sandbox: plan, diff, approve, merge. Approval and destructive moments are labelled, never colour alone.
04
Soft, light, local
Squircle corners, floating surfaces, glass that switches off without a GPU. Fonts ship in the ISO.
